Is the break per club or per team?

e.g. "Club A is off 12:00–13:00" — does that apply to all of Club A's teams, or can individual teams have different break times?

A

Per club — all teams in a club share the same break

Simpler to configure. Club A has one lunch slot, Club B has another. Configured once on the club, applies to all its teams.

B

Per team — each team sets its own break independently

More flexible, but more fields to fill in. Useful if teams from the same club travel separately.
